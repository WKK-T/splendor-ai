# Artwork

The card artwork is a set of AI-generated architectural landscapes prepared for this project. The atlas contains 15 cells; the 55 separate WebP images complete the 70-image palette. These are presentation assets, not reproductions of the commercial game artwork.

The five gem companions and their two silhouettes are original geometric SVG illustrations included as source. Their colours and names describe minerals; they are not character artwork.

The two `.bin` files contain the frozen browser policy weights. Their identities and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in the adjacent JSON files and `../model-release.json`. They are not artwork.
